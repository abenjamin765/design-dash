# Object Library Index
Last updated: 2026-08-23 (eet-coach-console audit)

| Slug | Name | Status | Dash | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `coach` | Coach | active | eet | A professional user who owns Groups, sets member Macro Targets, approves Meal Plans, and reads Weekly Summaries. |
| `group` | Group | active | eet | A coach-owned container of members that scopes visibility to that coach and its members. |
| `macro-targets` | Macro Targets | active | eet | Coach-set daily nutrition goals a member plans against. |
| `meal-log-entry` | Meal Log Entry | active | eet | The member's daily completion mark on a Planned Meal: as-is, modified, or replaced. |
| `meal-plan` | Meal Plan | active | eet | A member's dated Monday–Sunday set of Planned Meals for one week — the app's central weekly object. |
| `member` | Member | active | eet | A person on a nutrition program who drafts weekly meal plans, submits them for coach approval, and logs daily execution. |
| `plan-review` | Plan Review | active | eet | The record of a Coach's decision on a submitted Meal Plan: approve, or request changes with feedback. |
| `planned-meal` | Planned Meal | active | eet | One meal slot on one day inside a Meal Plan, with a food/portion list and auto-summed macros. |
| `weekly-summary` | Weekly Summary | active | eet | The computed end-of-week activity digest for one Group. |
