# Requirements excerpt — Team reading list

**Dash slug:** reading-list
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Tier:** Express
**Required gate:** edge_ethics_equity
**Status:** Excerpt for the canonical example

**Synthetic example.** The problem statement uses the synthetic tally in [assumptions.md](assumptions.md). Do not treat it as research.

## Problem

Team members recommend books and articles in chat, then cannot find them. Eight people were asked, in a synthetic tally, whether they had lost a recommendation in the last month; six said yes (A-001, reported, confidence M). The cost of doing nothing is another month of the same chat scroll. The outcome is one shared list a team member can open and update.

## In scope

- One list for one team.
- Add a title with an optional note and a status: To read, Reading, or Finished.
- Show the list, including the empty list.
- If a save fails, keep the draft and leave the list unchanged.
- Remove a title in one labeled action.

## Out of scope

- Accounts, names, email, or “suggested by.”
- Private lists, topics, comments, reactions, and streaks.
- Recommendations generated by a ranking system.
- A second role, or a rollout past this team (either one leaves Express).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a team member and a title they can name, when they save it, then the list shows that title with its note and status.
- Given a list with no titles, when a team member opens it, then they see “No titles yet,” the reason to add one, and an empty add form. No fake saved titles appear.
- Given a filled add form, when the save fails, then the draft remains, the new title is absent from the list, and the page says the list did not change and to try again.
- Given a saved title, when a team member chooses Remove, then that title and its note leave the list. The page does not ask for a name or an account.

## Ethics floor

This is the Express gate. Decisions, not a bare pass:

- No countdown, streak, or badge. Status describes the title, not the person.
- Remove is labeled Remove and sits on the row. Adding is not easier than stopping.
- The form asks for a title, an optional note, and a status. It does not ask who you are.
- Labels are “Title,” “Note,” “To read,” “Reading,” and “Finished.”
- Audience is adult teammates. No minors. Section C did not flag, so the tier stays Express.

## Learning note

The Learning gate is deferred (A-003). This note is the debt, not a closed gate.

The cheapest next check: after two weeks, count titles whose status moved from To read to Reading or Finished. The list does not store who added a title. If every title stays at To read, the list is a write-only dump and A-001’s second half (“they will look them up”) fails. That check has not been run. The tally that confirmed A-001 was synthetic and too small to close the question.

Run the check, or escalate the tier, before the list is offered to a second team.
